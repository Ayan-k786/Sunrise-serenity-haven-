About Sunrise Serenity Haven

A private retreat made for togetherness.

Sunrise Serenity Haven is a 4 BHK private villa in Tungarli, Lonavala, created for guests who want space, comfort and privacy under one roof. Whether it is a family holiday, birthday, celebration, friends' weekend, corporate stay or small gathering, the villa gives your group room to create its own moments.

With a private pool, indoor entertainment, BBQ space and comfortable bedrooms, every part of the stay is designed around easy, memorable living.
